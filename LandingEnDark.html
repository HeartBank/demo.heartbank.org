Why start a Family Kitty℠

Everyday help gets noticed

A thank-you for the cooking, the caring and the tidying-up can come with a small amount of real money.

One shared place

The whole family can see thanks going in and coming out.

Children learn to pass it on

When a child is thanked, half is theirs to keep and half is set aside to give forward.

You stay in charge

A parent or guardian opens it, invites the family and decides who joins.

What it does

  • Every thank-you is written down where your family can see it.
  • Money moves between your own accounts. HeartBank never holds it.
  • Thanks can be passed on: what you receive, you can give forward.

Three things in the app

Family Kitty℠

One shared place for a family's thank-yous and gifts.

Personal Account℠

Your own place to give and to receive.

Re-Thank Foundation℠

Where a child learns to pass thanks on. Yours to give, never yours to keep.

What it is, and what it is not

HeartBank keeps the record. Your own bank accounts and the KHQR code move the money. This page takes no payments.
